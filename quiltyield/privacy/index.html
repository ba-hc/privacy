QuiltYield privacy policy

Effective 10 October 2026

In short

QuiltYield works offline. It has no account, no ads, no analytics and no servers of its own. It does not collect, sell or share personal data, and the developer never receives what you enter.

What stays on your device

Everything you enter is kept in the app's private storage on this device. Uninstalling the app deletes it.

Files you export

PDFs, spreadsheets and backups are made only when you ask, and go only where you send them: a folder, a cloud drive or an app you choose. That service then handles the file under its own terms.

Android backup

If Android's device backup is switched on, Android may include the app's data in your Google account backup, encrypted, under Google's terms. Your Pro purchase is never restored this way; it comes from Google Play.

Purchases

The Pro unlock is sold through Google Play. Google handles the payment; the app only learns whether the Google account on this device owns Pro. Payment details are never visible to the app or the developer.

Permissions

The app asks for no special permissions.

Children

QuiltYield is a general-audience tool and is not directed at children under 13.

Changes and contact

A changed policy is published at the same address with a new date. Questions: use the developer contact on the app's Google Play page.
